We're Getting the Class Back Together

Fifty years since we walked the halls at South. We're gathering for one night at the Redondo Beach Hotel to catch up, tell the old stories, and see who we've all become. Whether it's been five years or fifty since you've seen the old crew — you belong in the room. And if a few night owls want to keep it going afterward — we won't argue.

📸 Share Your Photos

We're building a slideshow for the night, and we want them all — not just the '70s, but every decade since. Senior portraits, old beach days, weddings, kids, reunions, last summer's barbecue — the whole fifty years. Any classmate can send photos.

Email yours to southhigh77@gmail.com and please include:

  • Your name so we know who sent it
  • Who's in it names if you remember them
  • About when the year or era it's from
